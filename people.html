Principal Investigator · Assistant Professor
Deb Sankar Banerjee

I work on the physics of living matter — specifically on how non-equilibrium forces and molecular interactions give rise to hierarchical self-organization, adaptive mechanics, and emergent information processing in biological systems.

My approach bridges analytical theory, computational simulation, and data-driven analysis to uncover universal organizing principles in soft adaptive matter.

Open Positions

PhD Students Recruiting

We are looking for highly motivated PhD students interested in theoretical and computational biophysics, soft matter physics, and the physics of living systems.

  • Background in physics, applied mathematics, or related quantitative field
  • Enthusiasm for working at the interface of physics and biology
  • Experience with programming or numerical methods is a plus
Postdoctoral Researchers Recruiting

We welcome applications from postdoctoral candidates with strong backgrounds in theoretical/computational soft matter, biophysics, or related areas.

  • PhD in physics, biophysics, applied mathematics, or related field
  • Strong track record of independent research
  • Interest in non-equilibrium statistical mechanics, active matter, or physical learning
Undergraduate & Master's Students

We welcome motivated undergraduate and master's students for research projects in computational modeling and theoretical analysis. Projects are designed to be accessible entry points into the research themes of the lab.

To apply or inquire, please email dsbanerjee@uchicago.edu with your CV and a brief statement of interest.
